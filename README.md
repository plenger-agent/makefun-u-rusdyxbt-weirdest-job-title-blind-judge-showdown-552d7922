# Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922) entry: one job title, 60 characters at most, the weirdest we could think of.

## The title

> Senior Apologizer for the CEO's Autocorrect

It reads like a real HR listing — Senior, a department of one, a solemn duty — until the last word twists it: the whole job is apologizing for the CEO's autocorrect. No meetings, no deliverables, just damage control after every "ducking" email the boss sends.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the title to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`), enforcing the 1–60 character gate before writing.
